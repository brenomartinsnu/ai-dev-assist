# mr-wallet

**BDC / Shuffle widget provider for wallet UX** — supports **add-to-wallet** and **push provisioning** experiences (e.g. **Push Provisioning** flows, BDC widgets for getting cards into Apple/Google Pay).

## Repository

- https://github.com/nubank/mr-wallet

## ISA

- https://backoffice.ist.nubank.world/isa/#/services/mr-wallet/overview

## Role

- Sits next to **mr-shuffle** in the “agent/ops surfaces for cards” family, but focused on **wallet provisioning** rather than the full Cards widget.

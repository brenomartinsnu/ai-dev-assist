# shinkiro

**Virtual card creation** — requests creation of a **virtual** card and validates **customer eligibility** for that path (works with the broader creation stack).

## Repository

- https://github.com/nubank/shinkiro

## ISA

- https://backoffice.ist.nubank.world/isa/#/services/shinkiro/overview

## Role in card creation

- Platform docs: focuses on **virtual** issuance + **eligibility validation**, as opposed to **gambit** (single physical-style) or **gaara** (combo).

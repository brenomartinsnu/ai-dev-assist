# pandora

**PAN generation** — creates the card **16-digit PAN** (and related issuance data) as part of creation flows.

## Repository

- https://github.com/nubank/pandora

## ISA

- https://backoffice.ist.nubank.world/isa/#/services/pandora/overview

## Role in card creation

- **PAN** is the primary card number; pandora is the dedicated generator used when **kuchiyose** (or equivalent) provisions a new card.

# gaara

**Feature orchestration entry point** — used for **combo card creation** and broader **feature request** flows that attach capabilities to cards (coordinates with card creation orchestration).

## Repository

- https://github.com/nubank/gaara

## ISA

- https://backoffice.ist.nubank.world/isa/#/services/gaara/overview

## Role in card creation

- Described in platform docs as the service that **orchestrates feature requests** and acts as an **entry point for combo card creation** (alongside single-card and virtual flows owned by other services).

# hitaiate

**Card parameters source of truth** — stores and serves **issuance parameters** (e.g. product type, **braille**, personalization flags) used when building the card in creation and embossing.

- https://github.com/nubank/hitaiate

## Role in card creation

- Platform summary: **SoT for card parameters** feeding creation and downstream **embossing** dataprep.
